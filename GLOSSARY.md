# Glossary

## Site Feedback

A message any visitor leaves about the website itself: a problem they hit or an idea to make it better. Arabic: "ملاحظات على الموقع". Not about events or the club in general; those go through the contact page.

Each piece of Site Feedback is one of two **kinds**:

- **Bug report**: something on the site is broken, wrong or confusing.
- **Suggestion**: a new feature or an improvement to an existing one.

Site Feedback is anonymous unless the visitor chooses to leave a name or a way to reply.

## Feedback status

Where a piece of Site Feedback stands for the admins:

- **New**: nobody has looked at it yet.
- **Seen**: an admin has read it.
- **Done**: it was acted on or turned into a ticket, or no action is needed.
