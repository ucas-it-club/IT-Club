# Glossary

## Site Feedback

A message any visitor leaves about the website itself: a problem they hit or an idea to make it better. Arabic: "ملاحظات على الموقع". Not about events or the club in general; those go through the contact page.

Each piece of Site Feedback is one of two **kinds**:

- **Bug report**: something on the site is broken, wrong or confusing.
- **Suggestion**: a new feature or an improvement to an existing one.

Site Feedback is anonymous unless the visitor chooses to leave a name or a way to reply.

## Feedback status

Where a piece of Site Feedback stands for the admins:

- **New**: nobody has looked at it yet.
- **Seen**: an admin has read it.
- **Done**: it was acted on or turned into a ticket, or no action is needed.

## Event timing

Where an Event stands in time, worked out from its date, start time and duration. Nobody sets it by hand.

- **Upcoming**: the Event has not started yet. Visitors can register if registration is on.
- **Running**: the Event has started and has not ended.
- **Past**: the Event has ended. Registration is closed and the Event belongs in the archive.
_Avoid_: finished, old, expired

## Story slider

The rotating strip of large image tiles at the top of the home page and of the events and news pages. Each tile shows one Event or one news item: its cover image, a date and a title, with a link to the full page. Only Upcoming and Running Events appear in it. When there are none, the most recent Past Event appears with a "Past" mark.
_Avoid_: carousel, reel, banner

## Membership

**Member**:
A student who belongs to the club, past or present. Identified by university student ID. Has a status: active, inactive, alumni or withdrawn. Only some Members appear on the Team page. Members never edit their own record; admins enter and change it, starting from the Member's Committee application.
_Avoid_: user, volunteer, team member (the Team page is a view of Members, not a separate group)

**Committee**:
One of the three standing groups every active Member belongs to: Activities, Relations, Media.
_Avoid_: team, department

**Development team**:
A small hand-picked group drawn from Members of the Committees. Not a Committee; nobody applies to it.
_Avoid_: development committee

**Committee application**:
A request from a student to join a Committee. When an admin accepts it, the student becomes a Member.
_Avoid_: join request, registration

**Event registration**:
A visitor signing up to attend one Event.
_Avoid_: application, signup sheet

**Contribution**:
One piece of work a Member did for one Event or activity, such as organizing, presenting, design or media coverage. Recorded and approved by admins, never by the Member. Visible to admins only, never on the public site.
_Avoid_: achievement (an Achievement is a club-level result shown on the site)
